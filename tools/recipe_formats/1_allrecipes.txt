Easy Chicken Cacciatore
Prep Time: 15 mins  Cook Time: 45 mins  Servings: 6

Ingredients
2 tablespoons olive oil
2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs
1 (14.5 ounce) can diced tomatoes, undrained
1 (8 ounce) package sliced fresh mushrooms
½ cup dry red wine
¼ cup chopped fresh parsley
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 teaspoon dried oregano
Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Directions
Step 1
Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook until browned, about 5 minutes per side.
Step 2
Stir in tomatoes, mushrooms, wine, parsley, garlic and oregano. Simmer 30 minutes.
