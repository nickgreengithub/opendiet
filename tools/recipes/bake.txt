Vanilla sponge cake
Makes 8

225g unsalted butter, softened
225g caster sugar
4 large eggs
225g self raising flour
1 tsp baking powder
1 tsp vanilla extract
2 tbsp milk
Pinch of salt

FOR THE ICING
100g icing sugar
50g cream cheese
1 tbsp lemon juice

Preheat the oven to 180C and grease two 8-inch cake tins.
Cream the butter and sugar together until light and fluffy.
Beat in the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour and baking powder.
Stir in the vanilla extract and milk to loosen the batter.
Divide between the tins and bake for 25 minutes until golden.
Cool completely before icing.
Mix the icing sugar, cream cheese and lemon juice and spread over the cake.
