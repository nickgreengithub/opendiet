Chicken curry
Serves 4

FOR THE CURRY
2 tbsp vegetable oil
1 onion, diced
3 cloves garlic, crushed
1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
2 tsp ground cumin
2 tsp ground coriander
1 tsp turmeric
1 tsp chili flakes (optional)
600g chicken thighs, diced
400g tinned tomatoes
200ml coconut milk
1 tsp salt
Salt and pepper, to taste

Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat and fry the onion until soft.
Add the garlic and ginger and cook for another minute.
Stir in the spices and cook until fragrant.
Add the chicken and brown on all sides.
Pour in the tomatoes and coconut milk, bring to a simmer.
Cover and cook for 25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
Season to taste and serve with rice.
