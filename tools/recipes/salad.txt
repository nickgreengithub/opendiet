Greek salad
Serves 2

2 tomatoes, chopped
1 cucumber, diced
1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
1 bell pepper, sliced
100g feta cheese, crumbled
50g kalamata olives
2 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp red wine vinegar
1 tsp dried oregano
Salt and pepper, to taste

Combine the tomatoes, cucumber, onion and pepper in a large bowl.
Add the feta and olives.
Whisk the olive oil, vinegar and oregano together and drizzle over the salad.
Season to taste and serve immediately.
